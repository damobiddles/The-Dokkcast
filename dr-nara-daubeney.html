Dr Nara Daubeney

The Dokkcast is hosted by Dr Nara Daubeney, who leads accessible, evidence-led conversations with medical and healthcare specialists.

As a doctor, Nara approaches every conversation with clinical understanding, curiosity and a commitment to making reliable health information easier to access.

She asks the questions patients often want answered, while giving each guest the space to explain their subject properly.
