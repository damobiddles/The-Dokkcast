Coming Soon on The Dokkcast: Four New Conversations

By The Dokkcast · 27 October 2026 · 2 min read

The orange sofa has been busy. Over the past few weeks Dr Nara Daubeney has recorded a new run of The Dokkcast with four guests from cancer research, children's medicine and the charity world. Here is a first look at who is coming up.

Professor Susana Banerjee

Consultant Medical Oncologist, The Royal Marsden, and Professor at The Institute of Cancer Research

Professor Banerjee specialises in gynaecological cancers and leads clinical trials of new treatments. She brings expert insight from one of the UK's leading cancer centres.

Dr Yiannis Ioannou

Consultant Paediatrician

Dr Ioannou cares for babies, children and young people, and helps parents make sense of the everyday health worries that come with raising a family.

Simon Daglish

OBE, Co-founder of Walking With The Wounded

A former serviceman, Simon co-founded Walking With The Wounded with Ed Parker in 2010. The charity helps UK military veterans with employment, mental health and wellbeing, and supports their move into civilian life. Alongside it, Simon has had a long career in media, including senior commercial leadership at ITV.

Jess Wright

Television personality and supporter of The Brompton Fountain

Jess first became a household name as an original cast member of The Only Way Is Essex. After her son was diagnosed with a congenital heart condition, she began working with The Brompton Fountain, the charity supporting children with serious heart and lung conditions at the Royal Brompton Hospital. She now hosts the charity's Have A Heart Gala Ball at The Savoy.
