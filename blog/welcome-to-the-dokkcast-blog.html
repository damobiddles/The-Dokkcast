Welcome to The Dokkcast blog

By The Dokkcast · 8 October 2026 · 1 min read

The Dokkcast is an evidence-led health podcast hosted by Dr Nara Daubeney. Each episode features open, accessible conversations with doctors, clinicians, researchers and healthcare specialists, who translate complex medical subjects into information you can understand and use.

This blog sits alongside the podcast.

What you will find here

  • Articles that go alongside our episodes
  • Background on the subjects we discuss and the people we speak to
  • News from the show

The same standards as the podcast

Online health information is everywhere, but trustworthy explanation can be much harder to find. Like the podcast, this blog puts clinical expertise and evidence before trends, personalities or hype. Where a subject is complex or uncertain, we will say so.

Everything here is general information and education. It does not replace advice from a qualified healthcare professional about your own health.
