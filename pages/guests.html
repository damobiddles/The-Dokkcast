Every episode introduces a guest with specialist knowledge or a valuable perspective on health and medicine. Many of our guests are doctors, clinicians, researchers or healthcare specialists.

Who we speak to

Guests are selected for their professional experience, specialist knowledge and ability to communicate clearly. We give each guest the space to explain their subject properly, to share what they have learnt, to explain why it matters and to consider where their field may be heading next.

How to get involved

  • Get in touch and tell us a little about you and what you'd like to talk about.
  • We'll let you know if it's a good fit for the show.
  • If it is, we'll share the practical details and agree a date.
