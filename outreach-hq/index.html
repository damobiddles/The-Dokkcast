0 of 6 done

    When someone says yes

    Thank them, then message Damien straight away with their handle. Don't promise anything yourself. Damien takes it from there.

    DM scripts

    Starting points, never copy-paste jobs. Every DM needs one specific thing about their account. That one line is what gets replies.

    The formula

    Hi + something specific about them + who we are in one line + one easy question. Under 60 words. No links in the first message unless they ask.

    Good comments

    A full sentence about their post. Not an emoji, not "great post", not about us.

    • "This is such a clear way to explain gut health. The fibre tip is one I'll actually use."
    • "Really honest post. More people need to hear that recovery isn't a straight line."
    • "Brilliant initiative. How can people get involved in the next one?"

    On TikTok

    Most creators only accept DMs from people they follow back, and the rest land in a requests folder. So on TikTok, lead with comments. DM only once they follow us back or clearly accept messages, and keep it to 5 a day.

    Who to contact

    Aim for a mix every day: about 6 people and 4 organisations.

    TypeExamplesGood size
    Influencers and creatorsNutritionists, personal trainers, physios, doctors and nurses who post, mental health advocates, running and recovery accounts2k–100k followers
    OrganisationsHealth charities, supplement and wellness brands, clinics, gyms, health tech start-ups, NHS-linked groupsAny size, if active

    How to find them

    #healthtips#wellness#nutrition#mentalhealthmatters#physio#womenshealth#ukfitness
    • Open an account you like and look at "Suggested for you" under their Follow button.
    • Check who comments on and tags our posts. They already know us.
    • Look at who follows similar accounts: charities, health podcasts.

    Skip anyone who

    • sells miracle cures or unproven treatments
    • hasn't posted in the last month
    • has big numbers but no comments (bought followers)
    • posts anything offensive

    Never message the same account twice in one week. If unsure, ask Damien.
