Who listens

The Dokkcast is for anyone who wants to understand their health more clearly. It is particularly useful for listeners who are tired of conflicting online claims and want thoughtful conversations led by qualified health professionals.

It is also relevant to clinicians, healthcare organisations and people interested in how medicine, research and patient experiences connect.

What we cover

  • Women’s health and hormonal change
  • Metabolic and musculoskeletal health
  • Voice and vocal health
  • Paediatric and specialist medicine
  • Mental wellbeing
  • Exercise, movement and recovery
  • Prevention and healthier ageing
  • Developments in medical research
Editorial standards

How we protect editorial trust

Credibility is what the show is built on. Every partnership has to fit our audience and protect it.

Guests chosen for expertise

Guests are selected for their professional experience, specialist knowledge and ability to communicate clearly.

Research and fact-checking

Each episode is researched in advance, with any claims that need verifying flagged. Names, titles, claims, disclosures and links are checked in an editorial review before release.

Clear partner disclosure

Where a partner is mentioned, the wording, the disclosure and a review contact are agreed in advance.

Review where agreed

Guests or partners can review an episode before release, where this has been agreed, within a set timeframe.

Reach

What every episode becomes

An episode is more than one upload. Each one is turned into a set of assets and shared across channels.

  1. The full episode, published as audio and as video where each platform supports it.
  2. A trailer for launch, with a short teaser and a call to action.
  3. Short clips in vertical and landscape formats, with captions, cut from the strongest moments.
  4. An episode page on this website, with a summary, the player, links and the transcript.
  5. Launch posts and email on our owned channels, with guests and partners given ready-to-share copy and assets.
  6. Targeted outreach to relevant media, newsletters and communities.
